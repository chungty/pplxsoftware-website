About PPLX Software.

I'm Thomas Chung. I run PPLX Software, a one-person company that makes TeamOps — PTO and onboarding for teams that already use Jira. No investors, no sales team, no roadmap held hostage by anyone else's quarter.

What we believe

  • Your time is the budget.

    If a tool needs a 30-minute demo to explain itself, it's the wrong tool. TeamOps installs in 60 seconds and the default config works for most teams.

  • Free until it's worth paying for.

    The Free tier is the real product up to 10 users — not a 14-day trial dressed up as generosity. You only pay when your team grows past that.

  • If we change the price, we tell you first.

    Founding-member pricing is locked for the life of the subscription. Public pricing lives in source code, gated by a CI check, and can't be quietly raised.

  • Your data stays where it already is.

    TeamOps runs on Atlassian's infrastructure. Nothing about your team leaves Jira. If you uninstall, the data goes with it — no export, no negotiation.

Who we are

One person. Background in HR, marketing, and operations — not engineering. I've spent years on the receiving end of HR software that promised to save time and quietly added work.

I build TeamOps alongside an AI engineering workflow that lets a solo founder ship like a small team. That means I write the specs, design the product, talk to every customer, and review every line of code. It also means there's no committee to slow down a fix you actually need.

Why we built it

Most teams under 100 people are running HR in spreadsheets and Slack DMs. They already pay for Jira. Their engineers, designers, and ops people log in every day. And yet PTO requests, onboarding checklists, and offboarding compliance live in a separate tab nobody opens.

TeamOps puts that work in the tool your team is already in. One source of truth for time off. Onboarding templates that don't get rebuilt from scratch every hire. An offboarding flow that catches the access revocations everyone forgets.

What we're not

We're not trying to be BambooHR. We don't do payroll, performance reviews, or engagement surveys. We're not a venture-backed platform building toward a 12-product suite.

TeamOps is the HR tool you use until you genuinely outgrow it. When you hit 200 people and need a real HRIS, we'll be the first to tell you. Until then, it's the layer that makes the tools you already pay for do the HR work you're already doing.
