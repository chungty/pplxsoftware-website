What it does

Leave management

PTO requests with manager approval flow, balance tracking, team availability calendar. Daily-engagement view (who's out today, who's out next week) — not the once-a-year submit-and-forget pattern that kills HR adoption.

Onboarding workflows

Five pre-built role templates (Sales, HR, Engineering, Executive, General Corporate) with 100+ tasks, automated due dates, manager check-ins, and progress tracking. Start a new hire onboarding in one click.
