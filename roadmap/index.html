Roadmap

What we shipped, what we're building next, and where TeamOps is headed. Vote on the features that matter to your team.

Recently Shipped v3.5.0

  • 9 Rovo AI actions with strategic intelligence across 3 personas
  • Employee onboarding coaching with pace comparison against team averages
  • Manager capacity analysis with Jira sprint integration
  • HR admin severity-triaged recommendations (urgent, action needed, trends)
  • Submit and approve leave through natural language via the Rovo Agent
  • Org structure queries — “Who reports to me?” and team hierarchy lookups
  • Country-aware holiday surfacing with public holiday detection
  • Anniversary and milestone detection for your team

Up Next Planned

Calendar Feed

Subscribe to your team's leave calendar from Outlook, Google Calendar, or any calendar app. Approved leave syncs automatically.

Smart Assignment Alerts

When someone assigns a Jira issue to a team member who's on leave, TeamOps flags it and suggests the backup. No more assigning work to people who aren't here.

Jira Issue Migration

Already tracking PTO with Jira issues? The Rovo agent scans your existing projects and imports your leave history into TeamOps in one conversation.

On the Horizon Exploring

  • Cross-project onboarding—tasks created in IT, HR, and Facilities projects simultaneously from a single template
  • HRIS integration—start with CSV import, then BambooHR API sync for automated employee data
  • AI template generation—describe a role, get a custom onboarding checklist generated by Forge LLMs
